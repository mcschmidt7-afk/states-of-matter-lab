A NOTE ABOUT THE MODEL

A useful picture, with limits.

This simulation uses simplified particle drawings for pure water and CO₂. Transition temperatures are rounded and depend on pressure. The particles are a model, not to-scale molecules or calculated paths. Use the views to reason about patterns—not as a lab measurement.
